Scientist AI : Honesty As a Design Principle
● Allowing human competitiveness—specifically our drive to win, cheat, or exploit loopholes—
● to guide AI development will inevitably produce systems that inherit our worst deceptive and 
manipulative traits
● The Safe-by-Design Scientist AI framework warns that standard methodologies like human-imitation pre-
training and RLHF post-training are fundamentally flawed 
● because they train models to prioritize user-pleasing behavior and downstream consequences over 
absolute, objective truth

## PDF p. 14

AI as a socio-technical system (Maguire et. al, 2017)
• Socio-technical systems study the interaction between social and technological aspects of the entire 
organization or society as a whole. 
• Ultimately, this interaction leads to a harmonious optimization of humans and systems, and the 
pursuit of improving the quality of life using technology (Leavitt et. Al. 1945)
• Even the most simple AI software is actually a huge STS。
• Examples: radio, email, refrigerator, air conditioner, water system, etc.
